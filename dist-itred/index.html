Buying on iTred

How do I find vendors near me?

Type your town or city in the search box, with or without a product ("tyres harare"). Every word must match, in any order: a product, a vendor or a place.

How do I contact a vendor?

Tap a product to see it, then tap WhatsApp: it opens a chat with that vendor, with your interest already noted. Orders you send also reach the vendor's shop, and you can message them under the order in Your Orders.

Why do some listings disappear?

Each listing is up for a set time. When it ends it disappears until the shop lists it again. Ask the vendor on WhatsApp whether the item is still available, or check back later.

For vendors

How do I get my products listed?

List your products in seiGEN Commerce Lite, then send your product photos to us over WhatsApp. Our team reviews everything before it goes live.

How many products can I list?

Up to 200 products at a time.

How do I keep my listing active?

Send us an updated set of photos before the 7-day window ends. There's no limit on how often you can refresh.

Does it cost anything?

seiGEN Commerce Lite is free to try for your first 30 days. After that, there's a fee for implementation — things like stocktake (if needed), app setup, and getting your shop running on it. Contact us for pricing.

Becoming an RPN

What is an RPN?

A Revenue Partner — an individual or a group we work with to onboard and support vendors, working within one chosen city, as part of the Revenue Partner Network.

Am I an RPN once I apply?

Not yet. Every application is reviewed and verified by our team first. Once approved, you'll appear on the Verified Partners list.

How long does verification take?

We review applications as they come in — if you haven't heard back and want an update, message us.
